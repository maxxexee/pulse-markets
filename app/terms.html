What PULSE is

PULSE computes an attention index over a fixed basket of public figures and topics, signs each daily result, and publishes it. The site is a preview: it has no accounts, no deposits, no trading and no payouts. Any market specifications shown elsewhere on this site are examples of how a market would be resolved, not an offer to trade.
