How the number is made

Independent attention sources are scored separately. Each entity is measured against its own trailing baseline in each source, never against the other entities. Wikipedia uses the median daily pageview count over the 30 days before the epoch day. Hacker News uses the smoothed mean daily story count over the same window, because those counts are small and often zero.

score = clamp(50 + 12.5 * log2(value / baseline), 0, 100)

Attention exactly at baseline scores 50. Every doubling adds 12.5 points, so the scale runs from a sixteenth of baseline at 0 to sixteen times baseline at 100. Each source publishes the mean of its entity scores, and the headline index is the mean of the source indices that scored.
