How the number is made

Each entity is measured against its own trailing baseline, never against the other entities. The baseline is the median daily Wikipedia pageview count over the 30 days before the epoch day.

score = clamp(50 + 12.5 * log2(views / baseline), 0, 100)

Attention exactly at baseline scores 50. Every doubling adds 12.5 points, so the scale runs from a sixteenth of baseline at 0 to sixteen times baseline at 100. The published index is the mean of the entity scores.
